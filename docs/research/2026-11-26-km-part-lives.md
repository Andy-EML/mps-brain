# Konica Minolta replaceable-unit rated lives (research, 2026-11-26)

Method: KM Europe spec pages (official "Imaging unit lifetime" rows) for drum / imaging unit and developer; Precision Roller (US dealer, "Genuine, Estimated yield") product pages for developing unit, ITB, fuser. KM does not publish ITB / 2nd transfer / fuser lives on public spec pages. Service-manual periodic-replacement tables were NOT reachable (not found). All "dealer" figures are estimates, quoted as listed.

Confidence key: KM official = KM EU spec page; dealer = parts-shop estimate; not found = nothing reliable.

Caveats
- KM EU "imaging unit lifetime" figures are "up to" and model-specific; US dealers list different (often rounder, higher) numbers for the same part. Both shown where they differ.
- Dealer yield-to-product mapping was scraped from "all supplies" listing pages; each yield was attached to the product name directly above it. Spot-check before relying on a single number.
- No figure for the 2nd transfer roller was found anywhere reachable.
